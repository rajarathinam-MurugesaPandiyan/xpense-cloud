4 Frequently Asked Questions

Does deleting my account cancel my Google Play or Apple App Store subscription?

No. Store subscriptions are governed directly by Google Play or Apple for payment security. Deleting your Xpense Cloud account does NOT automatically cancel active recurring billing in your app store. You must cancel active subscriptions in the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & Subscriptions) or iOS Settings (Apple ID → Subscriptions).

Can an account deletion be undone or restored?

No. Deletion is instantaneous and permanent. All encryption keys, transactions, and cloud backups are destroyed immediately. There is no recovery grace period.

How can I backup my data before deleting?

Before deleting, open the Xpense mobile app, go to Settings → Data Management → Export Data, and export your records in CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or PDF format to your device or Google Drive.

Who can I contact if I need assistance with deletion?

You can reach our official support team at xpensecloud@gmail.com. We reply promptly within 24 hours.
