This Privacy Policy explains how Xpense collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Xpense mobile application and related cloud services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process only the information reasonably necessary to provide and improve the Xpense personal expense tracking service:

  • Firebase User Identifier (UID): Unique pseudonymized user ID to associate your synced records.
  • Email Address: From Google Sign-In or Apple ID to secure account access.
  • Display Name & Avatar URL: If voluntarily provided by your authentication provider.
  • Session Metadata: Authentication tokens necessary to maintain active cryptographic sessions.
  • Trip Contacts (Optional): Only when you voluntarily pick a friend via the system contact picker to record trip splits or debts.
Password Privacy: Xpense never receives, processes, or stores your external Google or Apple account passwords.
2

2. Expense & Financial Information

Xpense allows you to voluntarily record personal financial records to track spending habits:

  • Transaction amounts, categories, subcategories, currencies, and timestamps.
  • Payment methods (Cash, UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer) and type (Debited / Credited).
  • Descriptions, notes, and receipt invoice image attachments.
  • Category monthly budget caps and spending threshold alerts.
  • Trip management records (group members, payer attribution, itemized split costs).
  • Repeat subscriptions (recurring billing intervals, cycle alerts, and renewal dates).
  • Lend and borrow reminder records (parties, due dates, settlement status).
Financial Disclaimer: Xpense does not provide financial, investment, accounting, or tax advice. Your records are purely for personal organization.
3

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Xpense operates on an offline-first architecture using AES-256 encrypted Hive local database storage on your device. Local data includes transactions, trips, repeat subscriptions, reminders, categories, and sync queues.

Strict User Data Partitioning: Local database boxes are strictly partitioned per authenticated user ID. Data belonging to one user is encrypted and strictly isolated, preventing access by any other user account on the same device.

4

4. Cloud Storage & Synchronization

When cloud synchronization is active, your transactions, trips, subscriptions, reminders, and categories are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 / HTTPS connections to our secure Google Firebase Firestore backend.

Offline-First Sync Policy: Even when cloud-enabled, you maintain full offline capabilities. Unsynced changes are queued in encrypted local storage and synchronized automatically when an active connection is restored.

5

5. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information strictly to deliver and enhance the core expense tracking service:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain active session security.
  • Calculate spending analytics, trends, charts, and budget thresholds.
  • Compute group trip split balances and equal per-person shares.
  • Schedule local device notifications for repeat subscriptions and lend due dates.
  • Store and synchronize data between your device and secure servers.
  • Enable premium features and track subscription entitlements.
  • Diagnose technical crashes and improve app reliability.

Xpense utilizes Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to verify user identities. Firebase Authentication securely processes tokens on our behalf following industry security standards.

9. Third-Party Services

10. Text Recognition & ML Features

Where optical text recognition is used (such as recognizing transaction details from receipt images), processing occurs locally on-device using Google ML Kit. Xpense does not retain or transmit image data beyond feature execution.

11. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with trusted infrastructure providers (cloud database hosting, authentication) strictly necessary to run the application.

12

12. Data Security

We implement modern technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data:

  • AES-256 on-device database encryption for all local Hive storage boxes.
  • Strict per-user data partition isolation preventing cross-account access on shared devices.
  • Encrypted network communication via HTTPS and TLS 1.3.
  • Secure OAuth 2.0 Bearer token storage in system Keystore / Keychain.
  • Device biometric protection (Face ID / Fingerprint) with auto-lock timers.
13

13. Group Expenses & Trip Member Integrity

In shared Trip expense management, accurate financial accounting and dispute prevention are paramount.

Transaction Protection Policy: A group member who has recorded or paid for any transaction within a trip cannot be removed from that trip. This ensures an immutable audit trail and fair settlement calculations for all participants.

14

14. Device Permissions

Xpense may request the following device permissions to enable specific user features:

  • Notifications: Used to deliver timely reminders for repeat subscriptions and lend due dates.
  • Biometrics (Face ID / Fingerprint): Used purely on-device to lock and unlock the app.
  • Contacts (Optional): Used only when you tap "Pick from Contacts" to populate a trip member or reminder name. No contact lists are uploaded or stored remotely.
15

15. Data Retention

We retain your account and financial data for as long as your account remains active. When you delete records or delete your account, data is purged from active databases immediately.

16

16. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data and may permanently delete your account at any time inside the app via Settings → Delete Account, or via our web-based Account Deletion Portal.

Permanent Cloud & Local Purge: Confirming account deletion permanently purges your user profile, transactions, trips, repeat subscriptions, and reminders from cloud databases, and instantly empties all local encrypted Hive storage boxes in 0 seconds.

Permanent Deletion: Account deletion is irreversible. Your records and backups cannot be recovered after deletion.
