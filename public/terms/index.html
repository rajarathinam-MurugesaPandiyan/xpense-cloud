1. About Xpense

Xpense is a personal expense management application that helps individuals record transactions, manage budgets, track recurring subscriptions, split group travel expenses, and analyze spending patterns.

5. Local Storage & Cloud Sync

Xpense utilizes an offline-first architecture powered by on-device AES-256 encrypted Hive boxes. Cloud synchronization transmits data over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS to our Firebase Firestore cloud infrastructure.

8. Group Trips & Member Transaction Rules

To preserve accurate split math and audit records, a participant who has logged or paid for a transaction in a shared trip cannot be deleted from that trip ledger until the ledger is settled.
